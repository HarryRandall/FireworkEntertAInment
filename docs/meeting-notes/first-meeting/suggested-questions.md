# First Meeting — Suggested Questions

## Understanding the Domain
- Can you walk us through how a typical consumer firework show is set up today?
- What does the current purchasing experience look like — how do consumers choose fireworks?
- How are pyromusicals (shows synced to music) currently designed by professionals vs consumers?
- What electronic ignition systems are consumers using, and what data/formats do they work with?

## Target Users
- Who is the ideal user for this tool — casual backyard buyer, enthusiast, semi-pro?
- What level of technical skill should we assume the user has?
- How much time would a typical user spend designing a show?

## Product Vision
- What does a minimum viable version of this tool look like to you?
- Should the tool focus more on show design, the purchasing experience, or both equally?
- Do you envision this as a web app, mobile app, or something else?
- Should users be able to share or export their show designs?

## Firework Data & Constraints
- What attributes define a firework product (e.g. type, duration, colour, height, sound, fuse time)?
- Are there safety rules or timing constraints the tool needs to enforce?
- Is there a standard catalogue or database of consumer fireworks we can work from?

## Music Integration
- How tightly should fireworks sync to music — beat-level, section-level, or general vibe?
- Are there existing tools or formats for pyromusical choreography we should be aware of?

## Assets & Resources
- What video/animation assets will be available to us, and in what formats?
- Will we have access to real firework specs or product data from ICON's catalogue?

## Logistics
- What's the best way to stay in contact between meetings?
- How often would you like to meet or get updates?
- Are there any hard deadlines or milestones on your end?
