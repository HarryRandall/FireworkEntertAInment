## Consumer

1.  As a consumer I want to ability to visualise my fireworks show.

1. As a consumer, I want to have a choreographed firework show that is synced to a song of my choice.

1. As a consumer, I want to have a professional looking firework show with no pyrotechnical experience.

1. As a consumer, I want the ability to manipulate and customise my fireworks show using natural language.

1. As a consumer, I want to synchronise my show with a song of my choice.

1. As a consumer, I want the software to run well on my device (no lag/long downloads).

1. As a consumer**,** I want to input my maximum budget so the app only suggests fireworks I can afford.

1. As a consumer**,** I want to input my firing location size to ensure the recommended fireworks are safe to use in my space.

1. As a consumer, I want to view a list of fireworks used in my show, so I can purchase them from my choice of retailer.

1. As a consumer, I want to only utilise in-stock fireworks, so I can get them immediately after I am satisfied.

1. As a consumer, I want the ability to save and load my progress.

## Retailer

1. As a retailer, I want to allow my clients to view what their fireworks show will look like before they purchase them, so they will enjoy it.

1. As a retailer, I want to plug my database into this app, so clients can plan their firework show with the stock that I have and I can sell it all.

1. As a retailer, I want to be able to update my available stock, so users don't get disappointed.

1. As a retailer, I want to increase sales by showing customers what my fireworks look like. 

1. As a retailer, I want to inform consumers on safety protocols and technical aspects.

1. As a retailer, I want the liability from using this software to fall on the consumer.

## Manufacturer

1. As a fireworks manufacturer, I want this software to drive more sales towards my area.

1. As a manufacturer, I want the software to be able to support new types of fireworks I develop.

1. As a manufacturer, I want to provide retailers a service to help sell fireworks.

1. As a manufacturer, I want to pass on safety regulations to consumers.

1. As a manufacturer**,** I want to tag my fireworks with specific "moods" and "personalities" so the AI knows exactly when to use them in a song.

1. As a manufacturer**,** I want to input the exact burn duration and technical specs of my fireworks so the timing in the pyromusical is perfectly accurate.

1. As a manufacturer**,** I want to link my existing video and 3D animation assets to my products so consumers can preview them accurately in existing simulation software.

## **Persona**

<br/>

### **1. Consumer: Jack (The Party Planner)**

- **Demographics:** 35 years old, Event Host / Family Man.

- **Background:** Jack loves hosting holiday parties and gatherings. He is comfortable with everyday tech but has **no pyrotechnical experience**.

- **Goals:** To create a professional-looking, choreographed firework show synced to a song of his choice.

	- To ensure the show strictly adheres to his **maximum budget**.

	- To input his **firing location size** to guarantee all recommended fireworks are safe to use in his specific space.

- **Pain Points:** Professional pyrotechnic software is too complex, lags on his devices, or requires long downloads.

	- He often feels disappointed when he plans a show in his head, only to find the items are out of stock at the store.

	- He struggles to understand safety distances and technical specifications.

- **Key Needs:** A smooth, lag-free software experience that allows him to easily save and load his progress.

	- The ability to use **natural language** to manipulate and customize the show without needing to know technical terms.

	- A generated list of **in-stock fireworks **used in his design, so he can purchase them immediately from his preferred retailer.

<br/>

### **2. Consumer: Lily (The Creative Enthusiast)**

- **Demographics:** 26 years old, Amateur DJ / Audiovisual Creator.

- **Background:** Lily is highly focused on the emotional impact of music and visual aesthetics. Like Jack, she has no pyrotechnical experience, but she cares deeply about precise audio-visual synchronization.

- **Goals:** To choreograph a stunning visual experience perfectly synced to the beats and emotions of her chosen song.

	- To use **natural language** to dictate the mood (e.g., "Give me a massive gold explosion during the chorus"), letting the AI handle the specific product selections.

- **Pain Points:** Existing tools are too engineering-focused, which disrupts her creative flow.

	- She doesn't understand traditional pyrotechnic terminology or product names.

- **Key Needs:** High-quality, lag-free 3D visualization to preview her show accurately.

	- An AI system that understands the "moods" of the music and automatically matches them with the right fireworks.

	- A clear, actionable shopping list of available inventory to bring her digital creation to life.

<br/>

### **3. Retailer: Sarah (The Local Shop Owner)**

- **Demographics:** 45 years old, Fireworks Store Owner.

- **Background:** Sarah manages a local retail store and is always looking for ways to engage customers and turn inventory over efficiently.

- **Goals:** To **increase sales** by allowing clients to visualize what their shows will look like before purchasing.

	- To provide a service that helps sell the specific stock she currently holds in her warehouse.

- **Pain Points:** Customers often buy blindly and are either underwhelmed or unsure of how to set up what they bought.

	- Managing customer expectations regarding stock availability.

- **Key Needs:** The ability to **plug her store's database into the app** and frequently update available stock so users aren't disappointed by out-of-stock items.

	- The software must inform consumers about **safety protocols** and technical aspects.

	- A clear terms of service ensuring that the **liability **from using the software and firing the show falls entirely on the consumer.

<br/>

### **4. Manufacturer: David (The Product Supplier)**

- **Demographics:** 40 years old, Product Manager for a Fireworks Brand.

- **Background:** David focuses on developing new pyrotechnic products and creating marketing assets to help retailers sell his brand.

- **Goals:** To use this software to drive more sales to his specific product lines.

	- To provide a value-added service to retailers that makes selling his brand easier.

- **Pain Points:** New, innovative fireworks are hard to sell if consumers can't visualize exactly what they do.

	- Inaccurate simulations in other software make his products look worse than they are in reality.

- **Key Needs:** The ability to add new fireworks to the database, including **linking existing 3D animation and video assets** for accurate previews.

	- The ability to input **exact burn durations and technical specs** to ensure perfect timing in the users' pyromusicals.

	- The ability to tag his products with specific **"moods" and "personalities"** so the AI knows exactly when to recommend them during a song.

	- A built-in channel to pass on crucial safety regulations directly to the end consumer.
