GLOW is a B2B SaaS platform that fireworks retailers embed on their websites. It provides 3D product simulations and an interactive show builder so customers can preview fireworks, design custom shows, and plan purchases - all browser-based, no download needed. GLOW doesn't sell fireworks itself; it's a sales and marketing tool that retailers subscribe to (starting at $999/year) to help them sell more.

<br/>



Pyro City is a large consumer fireworks retail chain (the nation's biggest Black Cat distributor) with showrooms across ~12 U.S. states. Their 3D Show Builder page lets customers preview and arrange fireworks into virtual shows before buying. They sell fireworks directly through stores, so the show builder is a shopping tool tied to their inventory - you design a show, then purchase the actual fireworks.

<br/>



IGNITE is a physical wireless firing system (hardware + app) that lets you fire consumer fireworks from your smartphone or tablet instead of hand-lighting them. It's made by the same team behind COBRA Firing Systems. You buy reusable modules (i18 with 18 cues, i36, or the entry-level IGNITE Lite with 6 cues), clip igniters to your fuses, and either tap buttons manually or run a fully automated, music-synced show. They sell hardware and accessories directly - this is a real-world product, not just simulation.

<br/>



Hammer & Anvil is a consumer fireworks brand that manufactures and ships factory-direct to wholesalers and retailers. They focus on nostalgic packaging, competitive pricing, and product lines like "Firework Americana" (premium 500g cakes and reloadable shells) and "Sawed-Off" preloaded mortars. They don't sell direct to consumers - they're a B2B brand/manufacturer supplying the retail channel.

<br/>



Finale 3D is professional-grade desktop software for designing, scripting, and simulating fireworks and special effects shows. It's aimed at both backyard enthusiasts and world-class professionals, offering HD 3D simulations, automatic rack/address scripting, audio editing, site layout diagrams, and export to all major firing systems (including IGNITE, COBRA, etc.). It includes 50+ supplier catalogs with thousands of real-world effects. [finale3d](https://finale3d.com/) It's a paid subscription tool (with a free 14-day trial) - purely a design/planning tool, not a store.
