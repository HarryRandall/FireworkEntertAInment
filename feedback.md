Stakeholder Engagement
Your meeting minutes were detailed, included clear action items, and were generally well written. However, stakeholder feedback was not always easy to identify within the meeting notes. This would be much stronger if the feedback were recorded more explicitly under a clear heading or in a separate section, so it is easier to see what feedback was given and how it informed the team’s next steps.
Planning and Organisation
The team demonstrated good planning practices through the use of epics, milestones, PBIs, and assigned tasks, and it was clear that responsibilities had been allocated across team members. However, the sprint goals themselves were not clearly defined. It appeared that the team was working more from weekly goals than from clearly articulated sprint goals. It would strengthen your planning documentation if the sprint goals were stated explicitly in one place, so it is easier to see the overall objectives of the sprint and how the tasks, milestones, and backlog its connect to them.
Execution and Quality
The team is showing strong progress. They have created a demo of the app to gather client feedback and refine the MVP features more clearly. The repository also appears well maintained, with comments and definitions that demonstrate good coding practice.
Reflection and Improvement
The team is showing good reflection by using stakeholder feedback to refine their work and further clarify and improve the MVP features.
